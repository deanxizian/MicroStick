## Physical acceptance

- [ ] Flash the final tagged CI image at `0x0` and verify its full write hash.
- [ ] macOS discovers and pairs `Codex Micro`; ChatGPT shows Micro settings and reconnects after restart.
- [ ] BLE automatically advertises again after disconnect and recovers after sleep/wake.
- [ ] Front short press sends after 250 ms; front double click requests cancellation; a later single press still sends.
- [ ] Front 250 ms hold sends paired Mic press/release without an added Roxy ring.
- [ ] Side short press cycles assigned Agents; side 500 ms hold opens the menu at Approve.
- [ ] Approve, Decline confirmation/cancel, Fast, Fork, Agent 1–6, Plan, Back, Forward, and Sidebar behave as labeled with the default layout.
- [ ] All home, voice, menu, detail, confirmation, toast, 100%, unavailable, and all-slot states fit the 135×240 LCD.
- [ ] USB label appears only while physical USB power is present and disappears after unplugging.
- [ ] `MicroStick Microphone` provides clean 48 kHz mono input; BLE control remains usable with the Mac microphone after USB removal.
- [ ] PTT suppresses tones; simultaneous BLE, UAC, LVGL, and Roxy operation has no audible dropouts, visible stalls, or task-stack failure.
- [ ] Battery percentage is stable, the charge marker matches power state, and the full battery fill remains inside its outline.
- [ ] 7D usage matches the local Codex event, cache starts dimmed, fresh delivery restores normal color, and reconnect refreshes automatically.
- [ ] `MicroStickUsageSync` appears under its product name, has no Dock icon, and remains idle between events/heartbeats.
- [ ] Clean install, replacement install, default uninstall, and `--purge` all complete without leftovers.
- [ ] MicroStick requests only Bluetooth/session-file access; ChatGPT owns microphone/Input Monitoring permissions.

Unchecked items must remain documented as unverified; a successful build alone does not satisfy physical acceptance.
